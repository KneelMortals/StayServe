Questions before you begin

StayServe keeps the workflow explicit so students and owners can make informed decisions.

Do I need an account to browse?

Browsing and account actions are part of the protected application experience, so use an account to access listings and submit an enquiry.

Does a request guarantee a booking?

No. A request starts an enquiry. The room becomes yours only after the relevant owner and booking steps are confirmed.

How is my exact address protected?

Public listings show only the coarse location information needed for discovery. Exact location details are restricted to the appropriate owner and accepted student.

Can I block an unwanted conversation?

Yes. Blocking keeps existing message history available while preventing new messages and further booking progression between the two accounts.
