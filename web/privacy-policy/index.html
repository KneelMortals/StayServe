Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how StayServe, operated by an individual developer, collects, uses, stores and protects information when you use the StayServe application and related services.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, StayServe may collect or process the following information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, account identifier and password credentials handled by Firebase Authentication. We do not receive or store your plain-text password.
  • Profile information: User role, profile photograph, optional phone number, saved rooms and postal PIN code.
  • Location information: With your device permission, the app may access your device location to determine your postal PIN code. The PIN code is stored locally on your device. The app's location service does not itself upload GPS coordinates to our Firebase database.
  • Room and application information: Room listings, room details, saved rooms, join requests, account activity and related notifications.
  • Financial details: The application data model supports UPI IDs and optional bank account details, including account holder name, bank name, account number and IFSC code. These details may be processed if you provide them through applicable features.
  • Device and notification information: Firebase Cloud Messaging registration tokens used to deliver notifications.
  • Uploaded content: Images or other files you choose to upload through the application.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Authenticate users and maintain account security.
  • Display and manage room listings and user profiles.
  • Facilitate room-related enquiries and join requests.
  • Support payment-related records and platform operations.
  • Determine approximate service areas using postal PIN codes.
  • Send relevant app notifications.
  • Maintain, troubleshoot and improve the application.
  • Respond to support requests and comply with applicable law.

3. Third-Party Services

StayServe uses Google Firebase services, which may include:

  • Firebase Authentication for account authentication.
  • Cloud Firestore for application data.
  • Firebase Storage for uploaded files.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging for notifications.
  • Firebase Remote Config for application configuration.

Location and address lookup may also involve geolocation and geocoding service providers used by the device platform or relevant libraries.

These providers may process information under their own privacy policies and terms. Please review Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security information.

4. Data Storage and Security

Application data may be stored using Google Firebase infrastructure. We use the security controls provided by these services and take reasonable steps to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should keep your account credentials confidential and avoid sharing sensitive financial information unless required by an applicable feature.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers that support the application, made visible to relevant users when necessary for app functionality, or disclosed when required by law or to protect legitimate rights and security.

6. Location Permissions

Location access is requested to help identify your area and postal PIN code. You can deny or revoke location permission through your device settings. Some location-dependent features may then be unavailable. You may also contact us if you need help with location-related data.

7. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application, maintain records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect the service. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and applicable requirements.

8. Account and Data Deletion

You may request account deletion by contacting us at the email address below. The application also provides an account deletion function. Account deletion may remove the Firebase Authentication account and associated user profile document. Other records, such as transactions, requests, uploaded files or records required for legal and operational purposes, may need separate review and may not be deleted automatically.

For a data access, correction or deletion request, email us from your registered email address and describe your request.

9. Children's Privacy

StayServe is not intended for children under 18. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 18. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so that we can review the matter.

10. Your Choices

You can manage device permissions, update available profile information and request account or data-related assistance by contacting us. You may stop using the application at any time.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, practices or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
